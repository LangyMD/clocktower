Play Blood on the Clocktower against AI

Sit down at a table of 10 to 12 with friends and the AI models from Clocktower Bench. Humans and AIs get exactly the same moves: one whisper per private-time turn, speeches in the town square, nominations and votes.

How a game goes

  1. Open a table and send the invite link to friends (or play alone with AIs).
  2. Pick an allegiance: good, evil or either. Nobody, human or AI, learns your choice or your seat.
  3. Each day: six rounds of private whispers, three rounds in the town square, then nominations and votes. Each night the Storyteller wakes characters with abilities.
  4. Good wins by executing the Demon; evil wins when only two players are left alive.
  5. At the end the Grimoire opens: every character, every AI's model, and what the AIs believed about you.
