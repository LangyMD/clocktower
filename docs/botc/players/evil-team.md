# Playing evil

Strategy for Minions and Demons, summarised from community advice (see [../sources.md](../sources.md)).

## Before day 1

- **Pick bluffs.** The Demon gets three good characters not in play. Each evil player should pick a claim that
  fits the Outsider count and can't be immediately disproven. Coordinate privately on day 1 so claims don't collide.
- **Pick a story.** A bluff needs consistent information over several nights. Write it down (mentally) and keep
  it.
- **Decide on a plan for the Minion's ability**: Poisoner targets, Spy information use, Scarlet Woman cover.

## During the game

- **Blend in.** Talk to as many people as a good player would, share "information", ask good questions.
- **Use the truth.** The best lies are close to the truth; the Spy in particular can share real information to
  earn trust.
- **Create doubt, not certainty.** Frame good players subtly; a loud accusation draws attention to the accuser.
- **Protect the Demon without being obvious.** Don't vote in lock-step; sometimes vote against your team.
- **Kills (Demon)**: kill dangerous information roles and confirmed players; leave confused or wrong players alive.
  Consider framing: kill around a good player you want the town to suspect.
- **Late game**: count votes. At final three, evil needs the good player to be executed or no execution in a
  Mayor-free game to stay alive; plan who will vote for whom.

## Bluffing tips

- Info roles are strong bluffs but need consistent fake numbers; protection roles are easy but explain
  nothing; Outsider bluffs reduce suspicion but can conflict with the Outsider count.
- Be ready to "double claim" if a real player claims your role: one of you will be doubted; be the more
  convincing one.
- Use the Drunk and droisoning to explain away contradictions in your claim.
