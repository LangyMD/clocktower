# Glossary

Terms used by the rules, the community and this repo. Each entry is our own summary; the official
[Glossary](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Glossary) is the authority.

**Ability.** The text on a character token. Abilities work only while the player is alive (unless the text
says "even if dead") and sober and healthy.

**Alignment.** Good or evil. Usually fixed by character type, but some abilities change it (Goon, Snake Charmer,
Mezepheles, Cult Leader, Ogre, Fang Gu, Bounty Hunter's evil Townsfolk). A player's team is their alignment,
not their character type.

**Arbitrary.** The Storyteller decides freely (for example, the Pit-Hag's "deaths tonight are arbitrary").

**Bluffs.** Good characters not in play, given to the Demon (and to Minions with a Snitch, or to the Summoner)
so that evil players can claim them safely.

**Character.** What a player is (Empath, Imp). A player may have a different character from the one they think
(Drunk, Lunatic, Marionette).

**Claim.** What a player says their character is. "Hard claim" (stating it outright) versus "soft claim" (hinting).

**Day / night.** The game alternates; "each night*" means every night except the first.

**Dead.** A dead player keeps talking and keeps one ghost vote, cannot nominate, and loses their ability
unless it says otherwise.

**Droison / droisoning.** Community shorthand for "drunk or poisoned". The two are mechanically identical
(no working ability; the Storyteller may give false information); they differ only in what causes them.

**Drunk.** (1) The Outsider who believes they are a Townsfolk. (2) The state of having no working ability
because of an effect such as the Sailor, Innkeeper, Courtier, Minstrel or Goon. A drunk player is not told.

**Execution.** The day's single nomination-and-vote kill. Distinct from death: an executed player can survive,
and a player can die by other means during the day.

**Exile.** The Traveller equivalent of execution.

**False information / misinformation.** Information the Storyteller gives that is not true. Only allowed when the
receiving ability is malfunctioning (drunk, poisoned, Vortox, Fibbin and similar) or when the ability itself
says so (Savant, Dreamer).

**Ghost vote.** The single vote a dead player keeps for the rest of the game.

**Grimoire.** The Storyteller's record of the true game state: seats, characters, reminder tokens, deaths, used
ghost votes, red herring, bluffs. The Spy and Widow get to look at it.

**Healthy / sober.** Not poisoned / not drunk.

**Mad.** Trying to convince the group of something. "Mad that you are the X" means you must make players believe
you are the X; the Storyteller judges whether you tried hard enough. Used by Cerenovus, Mutant, Harpy, Pixie,
Bigwig and others.

**Madness break.** Failing a madness requirement, which lets the Storyteller apply the penalty.

**Minion info / Demon info.** First-night steps in which evil learns its team.

**Night order.** The fixed order in which characters wake ([night-order.json](../data/night-order.json)).

**Nominate.** Propose a player for execution. Each living player nominates at most once a day; each player can
be nominated at most once a day.

**On the block.** The player currently set to be executed at day's end.

**Pings.** The information a player receives ("my Empath pinged 1").

**Poisoned.** No working ability, as caused by a Poisoner, Pukka, No Dashii, Vigormortis Minion, and so on.

**Red herring.** The good player who reads as a Demon to the Fortune Teller.

**Register.** How a player appears to other abilities. Spy, Recluse, Legion and others can register as a different
character or alignment; the Storyteller decides each time.

**Resurrection.** Coming back to life (Professor, Shabaloth). The player regains their ability and vote.

**Script.** The list of characters that might be in play in a game.

**Starpass.** The Imp killing itself so that a Minion becomes the new Imp.

**Storyteller (ST).** The game master who runs the game and knows everything. Not on a team.

**Swing / three-for-three / worlds.** Community analysis terms. A "world" is a full hypothesis about who is
what; "three-for-three" is a group of three players who each claim and confirm one another; a "swing" is a vote
that decides a close day.

**Teensyville.** Games of 5 to 6 players or the small scripts made for them.

**Townsfolk / Outsider / Minion / Demon / Traveller / Fabled / Loric.** See
[character-types-and-setup.md](character-types-and-setup.md).

**Vote.** Raising your hand when the Storyteller's hand passes you during a nomination's vote.
