# Travellers, Fabled and Loric

Rules for these types are in [../rules/character-types-and-setup.md](../rules/character-types-and-setup.md).
Base-script Travellers are also covered in each script's page. Summaries are our own.


## Travellers

Alignment is set by the Storyteller when the Traveller joins.

| Character | Type | What it does (our summary) | Tags |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Beggar](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Beggar) | Traveller | Traveller. Can only vote with a vote token another player hands them; if a dead player gives one, the Beggar learns that player's alignment. Immune to drunkenness and poison. | voting-restriction, info-day |
| [Bureaucrat](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Bureaucrat) | Traveller | Traveller. Each night picks another player whose vote counts as three votes the next day. | voting-modifier |
| [Gunslinger](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Gunslinger) | Traveller | Traveller. Each day, after the first vote is counted, may shoot a player who voted in it. | kill, day-action |
| [Scapegoat](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Scapegoat) | Traveller | Traveller. If a player on the Scapegoat's team is executed, the Storyteller may execute the Scapegoat instead. | execution-modifier |
| [Thief](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Thief) | Traveller | Traveller. Each night picks another player whose vote counts as minus one the next day. | voting-modifier |
| [Apprentice](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Apprentice) | Traveller | Traveller. Night 1: gains a Townsfolk ability if good or a Minion ability if evil, chosen by the Storyteller. | copy-ability |
| [Bishop](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Bishop) | Traveller | Traveller. Only the Storyteller nominates, and must nominate at least one player of the opposite team to the Bishop each day. | nomination-restriction |
| [Judge](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Judge) | Traveller | Traveller. Once per game, when someone else nominated, may force that vote to pass or fail. | execution-modifier, once-per-game |
| [Matron](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Matron) | Traveller | Traveller. Each day may swap up to three pairs of players' seats. Players may not leave their seats to talk privately. | social-restriction |
| [Voudon](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Voudon) | Traveller | Traveller. Only the Voudon and dead players vote; the dead need no vote token and no majority is required. | voting-restriction |
| [Barista](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Barista) | Traveller | Traveller. Each night, one player either becomes sober and healthy with true information, or has their ability work twice, until dusk. They learn which. | droison-cure |
| [Bone Collector](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Bone_Collector) | Traveller | Traveller. Once per game at night (not the first) picks a dead player who regains their ability until dusk. | copy-ability, once-per-game |
| [Butcher](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Butcher) | Traveller | Traveller. After the day's first execution, may nominate again. | nomination-modifier |
| [Deviant](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Deviant) | Traveller | Traveller. If they were funny that day, they cannot die by exile. | execution-modifier |
| [Harlot](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Harlot) | Traveller | Traveller. Each night except the first, asks a living player; if they accept, the Harlot learns their character but both may die. | info-nightly, self-risk |
| [Cacklejack](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Cacklejack) | Traveller | Traveller. Each day picks a player; a different player changes character that night. | character-change |
| [Gangster](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Gangster) | Traveller | Traveller. Once per day may kill a living neighbour if their other living neighbour agrees. | kill |
| [Gnome](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Gnome) | Traveller | Traveller. Everyone learns a player on the Gnome's team; the Gnome may kill anyone who nominates that player. | info-start, kill |

## Fabled

| Character | Type | What it does (our summary) | Tags |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Deus ex Fiasco](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Deus_ex_Fiasco) | Fabled | At least once a game the Storyteller makes a mistake, corrects it and admits it publicly. | storyteller-error |
| [Ferryman](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Ferryman) | Fabled | On the final day every dead player gets their vote token back. | voting-modifier |
| [Angel](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Angel) | Fabled | Whoever is most responsible for the death of a new player may suffer a penalty. | protection, social |
| [Buddhist](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Buddhist) | Fabled | For the first two minutes of each day, experienced players may not speak. | social |
| [Djinn](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Djinn) | Fabled | Announces the special rule (jinx) between characters on the script; everyone knows it. | jinx |
| [Doomsayer](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Doomsayer) | Fabled | While four or more live, each living player may once publicly have a player of their own team die. | kill |
| [Duchess](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Duchess) | Fabled | Each day up to three players visit the Duchess; that night each learns how many visitors are evil, but one gets false information. | info-nightly, social |
| [Fibbin](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Fibbin) | Fabled | Once per game one good player may receive wrong information. | false-info |
| [Fiddler](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Fiddler) | Fabled | Once per game the Demon names a player of the other team, and everyone votes on which of the two players' teams wins. | game-ending |
| [Hell's Librarian](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Hell's_Librarian) | Fabled | Whoever speaks when the Storyteller has asked for silence may suffer a penalty. | social |
| [Revolutionary](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Revolutionary) | Fabled | Two neighbouring players are publicly known to share an alignment; once per game one of them may register falsely. | info-start, misregistration |
| [Sentinel](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Sentinel) | Fabled | The Outsider count may be one higher or lower than normal. | setup-modifier |
| [Spirit of Ivory](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Spirit_of_Ivory) | Fabled | Caps extra evil players at one (limits alignment-changing chains). | balance |
| [Toymaker](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Toymaker) | Fabled | The Demon may skip a kill and must do so at least once; evil gets normal starting info. Used for beginner-friendly or small games. | balance |

## Loric

| Character | Type | What it does (our summary) | Tags |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Big Wig](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Big_Wig) | Loric | Each nominee picks a speaker; until voting only that speaker talks, and they must be "mad" the nominee is good or may die. | madness, social |
| [Bootlegger](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Bootlegger) | Loric | Flags that the script uses homebrew characters or house rules, which are listed for everyone. | homebrew |
| [Gardener](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Gardener) | Loric | The Storyteller assigns characters instead of a random draw. | setup-modifier |
| [God of Ug](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/God_of_Ug) | Loric | One player wears the "Ug hat": speaks one sound at a time but votes twice; failing passes the hat on. | voting-modifier, social |
| [Hindu](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Hindu) | Loric | The first four players to die return as Travellers of the same alignment. | resurrection |
| [Knaves](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Knaves) | Loric | Two Storytellers run the game: one always lies, one tells the truth, and they may swap once at dusk. | storyteller-error |
| [Pope](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Pope) | Loric | Duplicate good characters may be in play, and may also be bluffs. | setup-modifier |
| [Storm Catcher](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Storm_Catcher) | Loric | Names a good character; if in play it can only die by execution, but evil learns who it is. | protection |
| [Tor](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Tor) | Loric | Players do not know their character or alignment until they die. | hidden-ability |
| [Ventriloquist](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Ventriloquist) | Loric | A nominee who is "mad" about being a new character during their nomination may survive execution that day. | madness, execution-modifier |
| [Zenomancer](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Zenomancer) | Loric | One or more players have secret goals; achieving one earns a piece of true information. | info-reward |
