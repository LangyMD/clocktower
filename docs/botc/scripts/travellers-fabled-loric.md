# Travellers, Fabled and Loric

Rules for these types are in [../rules/character-types-and-setup.md](../rules/character-types-and-setup.md).
Base-script Travellers are also covered in each script's page. Each row gives the official ability text and our
plain-language summary.


## Travellers

Alignment is set by the Storyteller when the Traveller joins.

| Character | Type | Official ability | In plain terms (our summary) |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Beggar](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Beggar) | Traveller | You must use a vote token to vote. If a dead player gives you theirs, you learn their alignment. You are sober & healthy. | Traveller. Can only vote with a vote token another player hands them; if a dead player gives one, the Beggar learns that player's alignment. Immune to drunkenness and poison. |
| [Bureaucrat](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Bureaucrat) | Traveller | Each night, choose a player (not yourself): their vote counts as 3 votes tomorrow. | Traveller. Each night picks another player whose vote counts as three votes the next day. |
| [Gunslinger](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Gunslinger) | Traveller | Each day, after the 1st vote has been tallied, you may choose a player that voted: they die. | Traveller. Each day, after the first vote is counted, may shoot a player who voted in it. |
| [Scapegoat](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Scapegoat) | Traveller | If a player of your alignment is executed, you might be executed instead. | Traveller. If a player on the Scapegoat's team is executed, the Storyteller may execute the Scapegoat instead. |
| [Thief](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Thief) | Traveller | Each night, choose a player (not yourself): their vote counts negatively tomorrow. | Traveller. Each night picks another player whose vote counts as minus one the next day. |
| [Apprentice](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Apprentice) | Traveller | On your 1st night, you gain a Townsfolk ability (if good), or a Minion ability (if evil). | Traveller. Night 1: gains a Townsfolk ability if good or a Minion ability if evil, chosen by the Storyteller. |
| [Bishop](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Bishop) | Traveller | Only the Storyteller can nominate. At least 1 opposing player must be nominated each day. | Traveller. Only the Storyteller nominates, and must nominate at least one player of the opposite team to the Bishop each day. |
| [Judge](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Judge) | Traveller | Once per game, if another player nominated, you may choose to force the current execution to pass or fail. | Traveller. Once per game, when someone else nominated, may force that vote to pass or fail. |
| [Matron](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Matron) | Traveller | Each day, you may choose up to 3 sets of 2 players to swap seats. Players may not leave their seats to talk in private. | Traveller. Each day may swap up to three pairs of players' seats. Players may not leave their seats to talk privately. |
| [Voudon](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Voudon) | Traveller | Only you & the dead can vote. They don't need a vote token to do so. A 50% majority isn't required. | Traveller. Only the Voudon and dead players vote; the dead need no vote token and no majority is required. |
| [Barista](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Barista) | Traveller | Each night, until dusk, 1) a player becomes sober, healthy & gets true info, or 2) their ability works twice. They learn which. | Traveller. Each night, one player either becomes sober and healthy with true information, or has their ability work twice, until dusk. They learn which. |
| [Bone Collector](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Bone_Collector) | Traveller | Once per game, at night*, choose a dead player: they regain their ability until dusk. | Traveller. Once per game at night (not the first) picks a dead player who regains their ability until dusk. |
| [Butcher](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Butcher) | Traveller | Each day, after the 1st execution, you may nominate again. | Traveller. After the day's first execution, may nominate again. |
| [Deviant](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Deviant) | Traveller | If you were funny today, you cannot die by exile. | Traveller. If they were funny that day, they cannot die by exile. |
| [Harlot](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Harlot) | Traveller | Each night*, choose a living player: if they agree, you learn their character, but you both might die. | Traveller. Each night except the first, asks a living player; if they accept, the Harlot learns their character but both may die. |
| [Cacklejack](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Cacklejack) | Traveller | Each day, choose a player: a different player changes character tonight. | Traveller. Each day picks a player; a different player changes character that night. |
| [Gangster](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Gangster) | Traveller | Once per day, you may choose to kill an alive neighbor, if your other alive neighbor agrees. | Traveller. Once per day may kill a living neighbour if their other living neighbour agrees. |
| [Gnome](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Gnome) | Traveller | All players start knowing a player of your alignment. You may choose to kill anyone who nominates them. | Traveller. Everyone learns a player on the Gnome's team; the Gnome may kill anyone who nominates that player. |

## Fabled

| Character | Type | Official ability | In plain terms (our summary) |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Deus ex Fiasco](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Deus_ex_Fiasco) | Fabled | At least once per game, the Storyteller will make a mistake, correct it, and publicly admit to it. | At least once a game the Storyteller makes a mistake, corrects it and admits it publicly. |
| [Ferryman](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Ferryman) | Fabled | On the final day, all dead players regain their vote token. | On the final day every dead player gets their vote token back. |
| [Angel](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Angel) | Fabled | Something bad might happen to whoever is most responsible for the death of a new player. | Whoever is most responsible for the death of a new player may suffer a penalty. |
| [Buddhist](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Buddhist) | Fabled | For the first 2 minutes of each day, veteran players may not talk. | For the first two minutes of each day, experienced players may not speak. |
| [Djinn](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Djinn) | Fabled | Use the Djinn's special rule. All players know what it is. | Announces the special rule (jinx) between characters on the script; everyone knows it. |
| [Doomsayer](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Doomsayer) | Fabled | If 4 or more players live, each living player may publicly choose (once per game) that a player of their own alignment dies. | While four or more live, each living player may once publicly have a player of their own team die. |
| [Duchess](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Duchess) | Fabled | Each day, 3 players may choose to visit you. At night*, each visitor learns how many visitors are evil, but 1 gets false info. | Each day up to three players visit the Duchess; that night each learns how many visitors are evil, but one gets false information. |
| [Fibbin](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Fibbin) | Fabled | Once per game, 1 good player might get incorrect information. | Once per game one good player may receive wrong information. |
| [Fiddler](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Fiddler) | Fabled | Once per game, the Demon secretly chooses an opposing player: all players choose which of these 2 players win. | Once per game the Demon names a player of the other team, and everyone votes on which of the two players' teams wins. |
| [Hell's Librarian](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Hell's_Librarian) | Fabled | Something bad might happen to whoever talks when the Storyteller has asked for silence. | Whoever speaks when the Storyteller has asked for silence may suffer a penalty. |
| [Revolutionary](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Revolutionary) | Fabled | 2 neighboring players are known to be the same alignment. Once per game, 1 of them registers falsely. | Two neighbouring players are publicly known to share an alignment; once per game one of them may register falsely. |
| [Sentinel](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Sentinel) | Fabled | There might be 1 extra or 1 fewer Outsider in play. | The Outsider count may be one higher or lower than normal. |
| [Spirit of Ivory](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Spirit_of_Ivory) | Fabled | There can't be more than 1 extra evil player. | Caps extra evil players at one (limits alignment-changing chains). |
| [Toymaker](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Toymaker) | Fabled | The Demon may choose not to attack & must do this at least once per game. Evil players get normal starting info. | The Demon may skip a kill and must do so at least once; evil gets normal starting info. Used for beginner-friendly or small games. |

## Loric

| Character | Type | Official ability | In plain terms (our summary) |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Big Wig](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Big_Wig) | Loric | Each nominee chooses a player: until voting, only they may speak & they are mad the nominee is good or they might die. | Each nominee picks a speaker; until voting only that speaker talks, and they must be "mad" the nominee is good or may die. |
| [Bootlegger](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Bootlegger) | Loric | This script has homebrew characters or rules. | Flags that the script uses homebrew characters or house rules, which are listed for everyone. |
| [Gardener](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Gardener) | Loric | The Storyteller assigns all players' characters. | The Storyteller assigns characters instead of a random draw. |
| [God of Ug](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/God_of_Ug) | Loric | One Ug hat. When wear Ug hat, must speak one sound at a time but vote twice. If fail, pass Ug hat. | One player wears the "Ug hat": speaks one sound at a time but votes twice; failing passes the hat on. |
| [Hindu](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Hindu) | Loric | The first 4 players to die are immediately reincarnated as Travellers of the same alignment. | The first four players to die return as Travellers of the same alignment. |
| [Knaves](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Knaves) | Loric | There are 2 Storytellers: one lies & one tells the truth. Once per game, at dusk, they might switch. | Two Storytellers run the game: one always lies, one tells the truth, and they may swap once at dusk. |
| [Pope](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Pope) | Loric | There are duplicate good characters in play. They might also be bluffs. | Duplicate good characters may be in play, and may also be bluffs. |
| [Storm Catcher](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Storm_Catcher) | Loric | Name a good character. If in play, they can only die by execution, but evil players learn which player it is. | Names a good character; if in play it can only die by execution, but evil learns who it is. |
| [Tor](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Tor) | Loric | Players don't know their character or alignment. They learn them when they die. | Players do not know their character or alignment until they die. |
| [Ventriloquist](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Ventriloquist) | Loric | If a player is mad as a fresh character during their nomination, they might not die if executed today. | A nominee who is "mad" about being a new character during their nomination may survive execution that day. |
| [Zenomancer](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Zenomancer) | Loric | One or more players each have a goal. When achieved, that player learns a piece of true info. | One or more players have secret goals; achieving one earns a piece of true information. |
