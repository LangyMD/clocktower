# Bad Moon Rising

The intermediate script about death and protection. Data: [bad-moon-rising.json](../data/scripts/bad-moon-rising.json).
Official page: [Bad Moon Rising](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Bad_Moon_Rising).

## What the script is like

- **Deaths are chaotic and frequent.** Four Demons with very different kill patterns (Zombuul, Pukka,
  Shabaloth, Po), plus extra killers (Assassin, Godfather, Gossip, Moonchild, Tinker, Gambler). Good has lots of
  protection (Sailor, Innkeeper, Tea Lady, Fool, Exorcist, Devil's Advocate on the evil side) and fewer clean
  information sources.
- **Information is scarce.** Few roles give hard information (Grandmother, Chambermaid, Gambler's survival).
  Good mostly deduces from **who died and who didn't**: the Demon type is the central puzzle.
- **Drunkenness is everywhere.** Sailor, Innkeeper, Courtier, Minstrel and Goon make the good team partly drunk,
  and many results are uncertain.
- **The Demon's identity matters as much as who it is.** Working out "which Demon" tells good how to play:
  against a Zombuul, a day with no deaths is good; against a Po, a quiet night means three deaths are coming.
- **Mastermind** and **Lunatic** mean even executing the Demon may not end the game.

## Script-level advice

**For Storytellers**:

- Track deaths carefully: night deaths in BMR have many possible causes; a clean Grimoire with reminders is
  essential.
- Use the protection roles' uncertainty (who is drunk) to keep games close, and avoid "nobody ever dies" games:
  if too many protections stack, the game stalls. The Assassin and Gossip are your release valves.
- Explain the Demons up front to new groups; BMR is much harder to read than TB.
- With a Lunatic, keep the fake Demon's experience convincing (fake Minions, bluffs), and remember that the real
  Demon learns the Lunatic's choices; the Storyteller decides whether the Lunatic's "kills" happen.

**For good players**:

- Identify the Demon type early from the death pattern: two deaths a night is the Shabaloth (or Po after a skip),
  deaths that lag by a night suggest the Pukka, no night death after an execution day suggests the Zombuul.
- Outsiders in BMR are dangerous to good (Tinker, Moonchild, Goon, Lunatic); a confirmed Outsider count is valuable.
- Execute to learn: with the Minstrel or Mastermind in play, a Minion execution has visible consequences.

**For evil players**:

- Hide the Demon type: a Pukka can claim to be a protection role, a Zombuul can fake its death.
- Use the Devil's Advocate to save a Demon from execution or bait good into a wasted execution.
- The Godfather and Assassin can make extra kills look like the Demon's, confusing the Demon-type analysis.

## Roles

### Townsfolk

#### Grandmother (`grandmother`)
- **Brief**: night 1, learns a good player and their character; dies if the Demon kills that grandchild.
- **Playing**: you have one confirmed good player. Keep the grandchild's identity quiet to avoid the double kill.
- **Storytelling**: pick a grandchild who can benefit from being vouched for. A droisoned Grandmother can be shown
  an evil player (a dramatic option).
- **Bluffing**: an evil Grandmother "confirms" an ally as good.

#### Sailor (`sailor`)
- **Brief**: each night drinks with a player; one of them is drunk until dusk. The Sailor cannot die.
- **Playing**: pick the player you suspect is the Demon or a Minion; if you are sober they may be drunk.
  Surviving execution suggests you are sober.
- **Storytelling**: choose whether the Sailor or the target is drunk; a drunk Sailor can be killed.
- **Bluffing**: popular Demon bluff, since "I can't die" explains surviving; it collapses if executed and dying.

#### Chambermaid (`chambermaid`)
- **Brief**: each night picks two players and learns how many woke for their own ability.
- **Playing**: checks claims (an Innkeeper who says they act each night should wake). Tells you if a "Soldier-like"
  claim is passive.
- **Storytelling**: remember who woke; the Lunatic wakes (as a Demon), as do Minions with night actions.
- **Bluffing**: hard to fake consistently; evil claims of 0 can shelter allies.

#### Exorcist (`exorcist`)
- **Brief**: each night after the first picks a player; if it is the Demon, the Demon doesn't act and learns the Exorcist.
- **Playing**: a night with no death after an Exorcist pick is evidence that you found the Demon (or protection worked).
- **Storytelling**: wake the Demon to show them the Exorcist; the Demon learns who you are, which puts you at risk.
- **Bluffing**: evil can claim to have exorcised a good player to explain a quiet night.

#### Innkeeper (`innkeeper`)
- **Brief**: each night after the first protects two players; one becomes drunk.
- **Playing**: protect yourself plus an ally, or two key roles. Be aware one of them will be drunk, so protecting
  an information role costs information.
- **Storytelling**: pick the drunk player to cause interesting confusion without wasting the protection.
- **Bluffing**: explains no-kill nights.

#### Gambler (`gambler`)
- **Brief**: each night after the first guesses a player's character; dies if wrong.
- **Playing**: guess confirmed roles to stay alive and "prove" information, or take a risk to confirm a suspect.
- **Storytelling**: a droisoned Gambler does not die from a wrong guess, which can make a lying Gambler look
  honest; decide whether to droison them with that in mind.
- **Bluffing**: survival "proves" a guess, so evil Gamblers claim safe guesses.

#### Gossip (`gossip`)
- **Brief**: may make a public statement each day; if it is true, someone dies that night.
- **Playing**: true statements kill; use the death count to test statements ("X is the Demon"). Use carefully with
  protection around.
- **Storytelling**: choose who dies from a true Gossip statement (good or evil) to keep the game close.
- **Bluffing**: evil "Gossips" explain extra night deaths.

#### Courtier (`courtier`)
- **Brief**: once per game names a character; that character is drunk for three nights and days.
- **Playing**: name the Demon type you suspect, or a Minion that is wrecking you (Godfather, Assassin, Devil's
  Advocate). Naming the Demon stops kills for three nights if right.
- **Storytelling**: if the named character is not in play, nothing happens; your death patterns may reveal that.
- **Bluffing**: easy to claim, hard to disprove.

#### Professor (`professor`)
- **Brief**: once per game resurrects a dead Townsfolk.
- **Playing**: bring back a confirmed information role or a strong protection role.
- **Storytelling**: a resurrected player's ability works again (single-use abilities already used stay used).
- **Bluffing**: "I tried to revive X and it failed" frames X as non-Townsfolk.

#### Minstrel (`minstrel`)
- **Brief**: when a Minion is executed, everyone else is drunk until dusk the next day.
- **Playing**: an announced Minion execution confirms it, and evil abilities fail for a night.
- **Storytelling**: announce the drunkenness publicly at the Minion's execution.

#### Tea Lady (`tealady`)
- **Brief**: while both living neighbours are good, they cannot die.
- **Playing**: if your neighbours survive an execution, they are protected and both good (or one of you is drunk).
- **Storytelling**: remember the protection covers execution deaths too.
- **Bluffing**: explains a neighbour surviving the night.

#### Pacifist (`pacifist`)
- **Brief**: executed good players might survive.
- **Playing**: claim it to explain a surviving execution; a good player surviving execution while you live is likely.
- **Storytelling**: your choice; keeps the game close but can confuse a lot.

#### Fool (`fool`)
- **Brief**: survives the first death.
- **Playing**: claim it when killed and you survive; it confirms you (mostly) as good.
- **Storytelling**: the Fool's survival is visible to all; a droisoned Fool simply dies.
- **Bluffing**: the Zombuul can claim Fool when it "survives".

### Outsiders

#### Goon (`goon`)
- **Brief**: the first player each night to target the Goon is drunk and the Goon joins their alignment.
- **Playing**: you may become evil; good players should avoid targeting you with abilities.
- **Storytelling**: alignment flips are told to the Goon; the Demon targeting the Goon makes it evil.

#### Lunatic (`lunatic`)
- **Brief**: believes they are the Demon; the real Demon knows them and their choices.
- **Playing**: you won't know; good players should treat a claimed Demon cautiously.
- **Storytelling**: give the Lunatic fake Minions and bluffs; decide whether their "kills" happen.
- **Bluffing**: rarely bluffed; evil may say a good player "is the Lunatic".

#### Tinker (`tinker`)
- **Brief**: can die at any time.
- **Playing**: claim early so a sudden death is explained.
- **Storytelling**: use deaths for pacing, often to break a stalled game.
- **Bluffing**: explains a lack of information.

#### Moonchild (`moonchild`)
- **Brief**: on death, publicly picks a living player; if good, they die that night.
- **Playing**: choose someone you suspect is evil; a good pick costs good a player.
- **Storytelling**: the Moonchild's choice is resolved that night.

### Minions

#### Godfather (`godfather`)
- **Brief**: knows which Outsiders are in play; kills at night after an Outsider died that day.
- **Playing**: claim an Outsider; push for Outsider executions so you can kill.

#### Devil's Advocate (`devilsadvocate`)
- **Brief**: each night protects a player from death by execution the next day.
- **Playing**: protect the Demon on the day it is likely nominated; protect a good player to bait an execution.

#### Assassin (`assassin`)
- **Brief**: once per game kills a player regardless of protection.
- **Playing**: use it on a protected or Fool or Sailor target; save it for a game-winning moment.

#### Mastermind (`mastermind`)
- **Brief**: if the Demon is executed (ending the game), play one more day; the next executed player's team loses.
- **Playing**: stay alive and credible. After the Demon dies, convince the town to execute a good player.

### Demons

#### Zombuul (`zombuul`)
- **Brief**: kills only on nights after a day with no deaths; survives its first death but appears dead.
- **Playing**: let yourself be executed early (you survive as "dead"), then play as a dead good player.
- **Storytelling**: track the "dead" Zombuul; the second death is real.

#### Pukka (`pukka`)
- **Brief**: poisons a player each night; that player dies the following night.
- **Playing**: poison information roles. Your kills lag a night, confusing who killed whom.
- **Storytelling**: remember the Pukka's poisoned player can be protected and survive.

#### Shabaloth (`shabaloth`)
- **Brief**: kills two players a night; may "regurgitate" one killed the previous night.
- **Playing**: a fast clock; evil wants an early advantage.
- **Storytelling**: regurgitation is your call; it is a balancing tool.

#### Po (`po`)
- **Brief**: may skip a kill to kill three next time.
- **Playing**: skip early to look like protection, then kill three at a decisive moment.
- **Storytelling**: three deaths can end games abruptly; good's protection can save some of them.

### Travellers

- **Apprentice** (`apprentice`): gains a Townsfolk or Minion ability on night 1.
- **Matron** (`matron`): swaps seats and restricts private talk.
- **Judge** (`judge`): can force a nomination to pass or fail.
- **Bishop** (`bishop`): only the Storyteller nominates.
- **Voudon** (`voudon`): only the Voudon and dead vote.
