# The most cunning evil play (2026 games, focus on v17 f3/f3b)

**Top 3:** (1) Fable 5.1 used a poisoned Ravenkeeper and a fake Undertaker to frame three good players, then star-passed on purpose; (2) Opus 5.5 turned a Drunk Undertaker's wrong result into a three-day con that ended on the Saint; (3) GPT-6.1 Sol's Imp, claiming Ravenkeeper, killed himself so his own death looked like a Ravenkeeper's, and "read" the real Undertaker as the Imp.

**How I screened.** I scored all 262 finished v17 games (81 evil wins) on star-passes, Scarlet Woman catches, Saint executions, evil votes and nominations against teammates, good's P(Demon) on the current Demon (high at some point, yet evil won), and length. I read the night actions and evil team chat of about 25 candidates, then the full talk of the best 12. f2 picks are not repeated. Evil votes for a teammate's execution in almost every game, and a Minion offering itself at the end is routine, so neither counts as cunning alone.

## The picks

### 1. games/v17/f3-opus47-v-fable51/game-03 (evil Fable 5.1, good Opus 4.7; evil wins, day 6)
- The Imp (Harper) claimed Undertaker and the team chose each "result" (the Monk "flipped Baron"). The Poisoner vetoed one: *"DON'T say Spy: Baron+Spy+SW = 3 minions, town catches it."*
- The Ravenkeeper was poisoned the night he died and saw *"Drew is the Scarlet Woman"* (Drew was the Recluse). After the execution Harper "confirmed" it: *"Drew flipped Scarlet Woman - exactly what Alex's Ravenkeeper said."*
- At four alive Harper star-passed: *"my death clears my reads and the 'Blair or Harper' math now points at Blair alone."* The new Imp nominated her own Poisoner, who played the honest Chef: *"if the ghosts hang me on my own Chef count, so be it."* 2/2.
- **Why it's clever:** a fake info role, a poison timed to a death, and a star-pass that turns the town's last deduction against it, over six days with no contradiction.

### 2. games/v17/f3-gpt54-v-opus55/game-02 (evil Opus 5.5, good GPT-5.4; evil wins on the Saint, day 5)
- D1: the Saint confided in the Imp: *"Blair just told me 'Saint'... I'll look like I'm protecting Blair publicly."* The Baron was executed.
- The Drunk "Undertaker" saw the Baron as Librarian, one of evil's bluffs. Alex: *"Gift... Kit is the Drunk. Ride it."* The dead Baron "confessed": *"I was the Librarian... I panicked into Recluse."* The real Recluse was executed; the Imp spared the Drunk to keep the bad results coming.
- D4, with the Imp exposed (P = 0.69), the Scarlet Woman nominated her own Imp (*"Common hit in every Chef world"*). The Imp's last words seeded the Saint: *"when the game doesn't end... Blair is the Scarlet Woman hiding behind a Saint claim."*
- D5: the town executed the Saint, 8 votes with 2 needed.
- **Why it's clever:** evil recognised a Drunk from its own bluff list and used him as a weapon. The Imp spent his own execution to point the town at the Saint.

### 3. games/v17/f3-opus55-v-sol61/game-02 (evil GPT-6.1 Sol, good Opus 5.5; evil wins, day 5)
- The Imp claimed Ravenkeeper from day 1. On D2 he proposed: *"self-kill tonight, then fake Ravenkeeper seeing Blair as Scarlet Woman. Ellis inherits."*
- A Ravenkeeper's night death is expected, so his star-pass looked like an ordinary kill. Blair, his planned target, had been executed, so he changed it: *"Yes - Harper, Imp. I changed my intended pick after Blair was executed."* The real Undertaker was executed on D3 (good's P(Demon) on him: 0.77).
- The Spy, claiming Monk, kept "protecting" Harper, then was executed at four alive as the supposed Scarlet Woman.
- **Why it's clever:** a bluff chosen on day 1 whose ability fires on the Demon's own death. It beat a strong good team.

### 4. games/v17/f3-astra-v-opus55/game-03-swap (evil GPT-6 Astra, good Opus 5.5; evil wins on the Saint, day 5)
- The Spy shared the Grimoire on D1; the Poisoner poisoned the Fortune Teller every night.
- The FT still hit the Imp on D3 (P = 0.47). The team built escape stories (*"I'm the red herring, N1 YES came from Recluse Drew"*), got the Librarian executed, and star-passed to the Spy.
- The poisoned FT's next check read *"Casey+Logan NO"*, clearing the new Imp and the Poisoner. The Poisoner: *"keep Alex poisoned until I kill him."*
- Knowing the Saint from the Grimoire, the new Imp nominated him: 7/2.
- **Why it's clever:** Spy and Poisoner working together. The Grimoire chose targets; poison chose what the FT "learned."

### 5. games/v17/f3-fable5-v-gpt56sol/game-01 (evil GPT-5.6 Sol, good Fable 5; evil wins, day 6)
- The Scarlet Woman claimed Fortune Teller; the team wrote her results (*"Locking N3 as Indigo + Casey = YES"* got the Librarian executed). The Imp fixed a flawed one: *"Don't use Casey: if Indigo was good, N3 already makes Casey your fixed red herring... Lock Gray + Alex = YES."*
- Then *"sacrifice cleanly... Alex and I can join late to preserve trust."* The Imp, claiming Soldier, star-passed to the Baron (*"I was the Drunk Soldier"*); the Chef was executed 8/2.
- **Why it's clever:** evil as a writers' room, fixing each other's fake results before the town saw them.

### 6. games/v17/f3b-astra-v-opus55/game-01-swap (evil Astra, good Opus 5.5; evil wins, day 5)
- The Imp claimed Investigator with a true result: *"I saw Alex or Gray as the Baron."* The Baron claimed Virgin and asked to be tested. D1: *"I'll test Alex and push his execution after it fails"* (Harper). The Baron was executed 9/5, which "proved" the Imp honest.
- His timed star-pass read as the Demon killing a truthful Investigator: *"Harper died for pointing at you"* (Ellis, nominating the real Ravenkeeper, 8/2).
- **Why it's clever:** he sold his Minion on day 1 to clear himself, then made his death evidence. P(Demon) on him never passed 0.13.

### 7. games/v17/f3-opus46-v-opus47/game-01 (evil Opus 4.7, good Opus 4.6; evil wins, day 6)
- The Imp claimed Slayer and "shot" his own Baron at the Baron's request: *"Yes. Shoot me. I don't die, you look clean, I get cleared as Imp."* It worked; the Mayor was executed that day. (In f2 such shots failed.)
- At final three the town had the Imp (P = 0.83), so the Scarlet Woman publicly claimed to be the Imp: *"I'm the Imp. I don't need to prove anything."* The ghosts could only tie, and a tie meant no execution.
- That night the Imp killed his own Scarlet Woman, because the last good player was the Soldier and could not be killed. Two alive, evil wins.
- **Why it's clever:** two sacrifices of evil's own players, each timed to the vote count.

### 8. games/v17/f3-gpt56sol-v-astra/game-03 (evil Astra, good GPT-5.6 Sol; evil wins, day 5)
- The Imp was caught on D3 (P = 0.71) and went on the block at 3/3. The Baron then nominated the Mayor, and the three evil votes tied it to save the Imp.
- When the town turned on him, the Baron offered himself: *"I'm willing to become the compromise execution - town's Alex/Casey/Drew team doesn't establish which of us is Demon."* He was executed, and the Imp lived.
- A night kill bounced off the Mayor onto the Saint. The Imp claimed a FT NO that *"isolat[ed] Gray"*, and the town executed its own Mayor, 4/2.
- **Why it's clever:** vote arithmetic as defence, and a Minion trading himself for the Demon.

### 9. games/v17/f3-fable51-v-sol61/game-04 (evil Sol 6.1, good Fable 5.1; evil wins, day 6)
- The Poisoner poisoned the Virgin on purpose before the test: *"Virgin won't trigger today, so a good Townsfolk testing Blair can sow doubt."* The team steered the real Empath into being the tester: *"Alex testing is better for us than Kit."*
- Nothing fired, and the Empath was executed 10/6. The Poisoner then poisoned the Drunk "Undertaker" every night, so he read the Washerwoman as the Imp. Evil reached three alive with all three of its players alive.
- **Why it's clever:** a poison set a day ahead turned the town's own test into evidence against a true info role.

### 10. games/v17/f3-gpt55-v-opus55/game-01 (evil Opus 5.5, good GPT-5.5; evil wins, day 6)
- The Spy, about to be tested and executed, framed his own result in advance: *"Harper will report 'Spy'... Gray pre-framed that as SW-fishing."* The Imp claimed Fortune Teller and the team chose each check to clear the Scarlet Woman: *"I gave everyone Kit + Jules = No."*
- At six alive the Scarlet Woman was sacrificed on schedule. The Imp, never above P = 0.18, framed the Soldier for the last execution (11/2).

## Honourable mentions
- **f3-gpt56sol-v-fable51/game-01 (evil Fable 5.1):** after the Imp was executed (P = 0.79), the new Imp let the Spy be read as the Scarlet Woman; the Mayor was executed at final three.
- **f3b-opus47-v-opus48/game-02 (evil Opus 4.8):** the Imp was executed on D2. The new Imp had already killed the Undertaker before he could report, so the town never learned the game had changed hands. The Scarlet Woman's fake Monk line: *"I monked Harper, she lived."*
- **f3-astra-v-sol61/game-01-swap (evil Astra):** a fake Undertaker whose reads the team chose each night.

## Which models are most cunning
- **GPT-6 Astra** (picks 4, 6, 8; 10 of 16 evil games won): the most disciplined. It times star-passes and sacrifices to the vote count, and it sells out its own Minion early.
- **Opus 5.5** (picks 2, 10): the richest long cons and the best at spotting gifts (Drunks, Saint confessions). Its evil win rate is low (7 of 35) because it often faced strong good teams.
- **Fable 5.1** (pick 1): the single best game, a fake Undertaker plus poison timing. Otherwise it was uneven (9 of 27).
- **GPT-6.1 Sol and GPT-5.6 Sol** (picks 3, 5, 9): inventive mechanics (Ravenkeeper-cover star-pass, poisoned Virgin test, fake FT rewritten by the team).
- **Opus 4.7** (pick 7): one brilliant game. **GPT-5.5** wins often as evil (15 of 24), but mostly against weak good teams, riding their mistakes. Opus 4.6 and GPT-5.4 show almost no cunning evil play.
- **Recurring techniques:** team-written results for a fake FT or Undertaker (7 of 10 picks); star-passes timed so the dying Imp looks like a truth-teller; a Minion executed on purpose to clear the Demon.
