How a game works

Ten to twelve seats, one secret Demon

Secret roles

Most players are good townsfolk with clues about who is who. Three are evil: the Demon, who kills each night, and two Minions who know it and lie for it. One AI model plays every good seat; another plays every evil seat.

Whispers, then the square

Each day every player holds six private conversations, one at a time, then speaks three times in the town square. Nobody can prove their role, so every claim might be a lie.

Nominate and vote

The town puts players on trial and executes one a day. Good wins by executing the Demon. Evil wins when only two players are left alive.
