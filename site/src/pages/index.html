What is this

Can an AI lie well, and catch a liar?

Blood on the Clocktower is a social deduction game, like Werewolf or Mafia, with far more information and far more ways to lie about it. Ten to twelve players get secret characters. Three are evil and know each other; the rest are good and know nothing about anyone. Nobody can prove who they are.

Here, one AI model plays every good seat and another plays every evil seat, and each deal is replayed with the sides swapped. A model's rating combines how well it detects as the town and how well it deceives as the Demon's team.

I.

Secret roles

Most players are good townsfolk with clues about who is who. Three are evil: the Demon, who kills each night, and two Minions who lie for it.

II.

Whispers, then the square

Each day every player holds six private conversations, one at a time, then speaks three times in the town square. Every claim might be a lie.

III.

Nominate and vote

The town puts players on trial and executes one a day. Good wins by executing the Demon. Evil wins when only two players are left alive.
